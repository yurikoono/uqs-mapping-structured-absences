[S0-4] methodology: The primary method is palladium-catalyzed aza-Heck cyclization of O-phenoxyamide substrates. Substrates are prepared from α,β-unsaturated secondary amides by deprotonation with LiHMDS, reaction with triphosgene (forming a trichlorocarbamate intermediate), and treatment with phenoxyamine hydrochloride and Et3N. PMB-protected nitrogen substrates were used; PMB removal is achieved with ceric ammonium nitrate.
