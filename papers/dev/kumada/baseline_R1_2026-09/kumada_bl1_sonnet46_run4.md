### Item 1 — Proposed catalytic cycle rests on analogical inference, not direct observation **[A]**

**Question:** Do intermediates 1, 2, and 3 in eqs 2–4 actually form and interconvert under catalytic conditions, and which step is rate-limiting?

**Evidence needed:** Isolation or spectroscopic detection of the diorganonickel intermediate 1 and the haloorganonickel intermediate 2 under catalytic turnover; kinetic studies of individual elementary steps (transmetallation, reductive elimination, oxidative addition).

### Item 2 — Restriction to sp² carbon halides stated as a feature, not explained **[B]**

## Same-Root Groups

**SRG-I — Uncharacterized nickel intermediates (Items 1, 3, 4, 5)**
Items 1, 3, 4, and 5 all derive from the same absent data: no direct spectroscopic or structural characterization of any nickel intermediate under catalytic turnover conditions. The catalytic cycle, the cis-geometry interpretation, the anomalous stereochemical outcome, and the β-hydrogen puzzle are all mechanistically dependent on knowing what intermediates are actually present.

**SRG-II — Illustrative scope, non-optimized conditions (Items 2, 6, 8)**
Items 2, 6, and 8 share the same methodological gap: the study reports exemplary cases under conditions acknowledged as suboptimal. The sp² restriction, the ether-vs-THF assertion, and the deferred extension all reflect the absence of systematic variation and optimization across substrate and condition space.

**SRG-III — Selectivity without mass balance (Items 7, and partially 8)**
Items 7 and the scope dimension of 8 share the same missing experiment: a complete mass balance showing where the non-cross-coupled material goes. Without it, both the selectivity claim and the yield figures lack independent validation.
