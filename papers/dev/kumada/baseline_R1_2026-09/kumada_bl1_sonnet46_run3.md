## Unanswered Questions and Unsupported Claims

---

**1. The proposed catalytic cycle is a working hypothesis built on uncharacterised intermediates** [A]

The three species in eqs 2–4 (diorganonickel **1**, haloorganonickel **2**, new diorgano complex **3**) are never isolated, spectroscopically detected, or individually trapped; the cycle is presented by analogy with a bipyridyl-Ni system.

*Evidence needed:* Isolation or in-situ NMR/IR characterisation of intermediates 1–3 in the dpe system; stoichiometric experiments confirming each elementary step independently; kinetic profiling.

**2. The exclusion of sp³ (alkyl) halides is stated as a substrate boundary without mechanistic rationale or experimental demonstration** [B]

## Same-Root Groups

**SRG-1 — Absence of characterised Ni intermediates in the diphosphine system**
Items 1, 4, 10. All three gaps converge on the same missing data: direct observation or isolation of intermediates **1**, **2**, and **3** within the NiCl₂(dpe/dpp) framework. Resolving this one experimental gap would simultaneously validate the catalytic cycle, explain the ligand-activity order, and confirm the transfer of eq-1 precedent from dipy-Ni to phosphine-Ni.

**SRG-2 — Absence of stereochemical mechanistic data on C–Ni bond formation and cleavage**
Items 3, 5. Both the tolerance of β-hydrogen alkyl groups and the anomalous cis enrichment in stilbene formation would be resolved by the same investigation: determining the rate and geometry of reductive elimination vs. β-hydride elimination from organonickel intermediates bearing the diphosphine ligand.

**SRG-3 — Undeveloped reaction-condition data**
Items 6, 7. The suboptimal-conditions caveat and the unsupported ether-superiority claim both reflect the absence of a systematic solvent/temperature/loading screen; a single optimisation study would address both.

**SRG-4 — Prior and concurrent Ni coupling literature cited but not experimentally engaged**
Items 8, 9. Both point to the same gap: no direct comparison experiment placing the new NiCl₂(dpe) catalyst alongside Ni(acac)₂ (Corriu–Masse) or the earlier Fe/Cu/Ag systems on an identical substrate, which would quantify the practical advantage claimed.
