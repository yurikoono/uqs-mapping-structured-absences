**Reading, applying the pre-registered rule: a difference is claimed only when the ranges do not overlap.**
- The UQS advantage over B0 and B1 is **explained by output volume**. When a single prompt is simply asked for a long, exhaustive
  list (B2), it recovers the benchmark findings as well as UQS does.
  - Broad level: UQS 7–7 against B2 6–7. The ranges overlap, so there is no difference.
  - Narrow level: B2 6–7 against UQS 5–7. The ranges overlap, so there is no difference, and B2's mean is higher.
- Where UQS still differs, it differs in **anchoring quality, not coverage**. B2 misreported a quote in 3 of 5 runs: an inverted

- **Consequence for Paper A**: the paper must not claim that the staged pipeline recovers more findings than a single prompt of
  similar length. The defensible claims are:
  - the staged pipeline produces structured, classified, cross-linked output (the A/B/C/U taxonomy, SRGs, D-4, EPs) that a flat list
    does not;
  - at matched output volume, the flat list had more misreported quotations in this small sample.

## Rubric-based adjudication (2026-09-29; rubric `adjudication_rubric_20260929.md`, frozen before rating)

**Who rated.** Two independent **Claude subagents**, blind to arm, rated 128 cells; they agreed on 128/128 at both levels.
The 12 cells with no candidate passage were read in full by the orchestrator (also Claude). **No human has rated any
cell yet**, so this is a model-based adjudication, not a hand adjudication. Agreement between two raters of the same model
family is weak evidence of independence, because their errors may be correlated. Rater 2 reported that for the
homocoupling, stereo and ligand axes it read keyword-filtered excerpts (about four per cell), not every snippet.
The two raters shared a scratchpad directory. Their verdict files were not cross-read (checked from the tool-call logs),
and the shared files contain packet text only. Author verification sheet: `adjudication_review_sheet.md`.

**Limits of this adjudication (Fable 5.1 review F4).**
- The two raters are the same model reading the same packets in the same order. 71/140 of their evidence strings are identical, so
  the 128/128 agreement is weak evidence of independence.
- The 12 "full-read" cells were in fact adjudicated with one identical keyword probe.
- Fable's spot-check found an arm asymmetry. Cell C062696 (bl0 run4, exotherm) quotes the Table I footnote "Reaction proceeded
  exothermally" and calls it "the only remark made about this mechanistically distinctive entry", yet it was rated 0/0, while a
  comparable UQS passage was rated 1. Cell C349650 (bl1 run3, homocoupling, narrow) is also doubtful.
- Re-rating these two cells as Fable reads them gives narrow loose miss rates of B0 40.0% (from 42.9%) and B1 42.9% (from 45.7%).
  The conclusion does not change.
- **The ratings are re-done together with B2 by two new raters, each with a separate working directory and an instruction to read
  every snippet** (pre-registration, B2 section).

**Consequence for Methods 6 (revised after Fable 5.1 review F5).**
- Only the **broad** level uses the same axis definitions as Methods 6 (the 2026-07-19 wording). **8.6% (strict) / 0.0% (loose)** is
  therefore the corrected value of the Methods 6 figure (5.7% / 0.0%).
- The **narrow** level is a different instrument: it drops the "radical mechanism" half of the exotherm axis and the in-situ Ni⁰ half
  of the black-precipitate axis. Its values (20.0% / 11.4%) must be presented as a second, stricter instrument, not as a correction.
- Under the narrow instrument, "omissions sit at the taxonomy-compression step rather than at the detection step" does not hold.
  The exotherm observation is absent from the whole run in 3/5 runs.
- Methods 6 must still not call this a hand adjudication. It is a rubric-based adjudication by two model raters, pending author verification.

**Output volume (Fable 5.1 review F6).**
- The UQS loose scope (s1+s2) is 48–59k characters; B0 and B1 are about 10k.
- A criterion of "appears anywhere in the run" favours longer output, so the B0/B1 comparison cannot separate the effect of the
  staged design from the effect of the output budget. **B2** (a length-matched single prompt, pre-registered before it was run)
  is the control for this.
- B1's definitions are one- or two-sentence summaries of the turn-4 definitions, not the same definitions.

## Reading (preliminary, before the adjudication)

A one-paragraph prompt on the same model recovers most of the easy benchmark axes (stereo, ligand ranking, ether/THF,
homo-coupling) and anchors its quotes as well as UQS does. The gap is concentrated in the axes that need reading beyond
the argument: the experimental-section observations (black mixture, the exotherm footnote) and the analytical basis of
the yields (GLPC calibration). This is consistent with the claim that UQS's staged viewpoints add coverage of
low-salience evidence, **but the instrument was built from UQS findings on this paper and has the false positives
above.** Both caveats must travel with any statement in the manuscript.
