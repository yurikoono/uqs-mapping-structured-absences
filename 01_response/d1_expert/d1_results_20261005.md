## Reading (descriptive; n = 10 items, no significance claims)

- **Primary agreement is low on the six categories and moderate on the collapsed scheme.** R1–R2 agree on 2 of 10 items (κ = 0.05) and, collapsed, on 7 of 10 (κ = 0.46). Over all four raters, Fleiss' κ is 0.19 (six categories) and 0.28 (collapsed). The six-way distinction is not used consistently across raters; the coarse worth-raising judgement is used more consistently.
- **Worth raising: 2–5 of 10 items per rater** (R1 5, R2 4, R3 2, R4 5). The 95% intervals are wide (about 0.1–0.75).
- **By item type (descriptive, 3 D-4 vs 7 Stage 1 items).** The three D-4 candidates were judged worth raising in 8 of 12 ratings; the seven Stage 1 items in 8 of 28.
- **Unanimous items.** Item 2 (β-H elimination vs high alkyl yields; D-4) was worth raising for all four raters, three of whom chose (ii) "already studied, but a good point". Item 4, the unquantified homo-coupling behind the "selective cross-coupling" claim, was judged (v) off-target by all four raters, three with high confidence. One rater's comment gives the reason: with near-equimolar reagents and yields up to 98%, little homo-coupling can have formed. Item 8 (GC vs isolated yields) was judged not worth raising by all four; a comment explains the mixed basis by product volatility.
- **Comments** (one rater wrote them; not linked to R-numbers under Amendment 3): summarised in `ratings/comments_summary.md`.
