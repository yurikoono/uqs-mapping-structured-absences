# Rater comments — summary (pre-registration Amendment 3)

One of the four raters wrote a comment on every item; the others wrote none. The comments are summarised here by theme, and are not linked to the ratings of any rater (R1–R4) or to any item's individual rating. Verbatim comments are kept in `../_private/`, which is not deposited.

- *Format of a 1972 communication.* Several items were read as consequences of the short-communication format of the time, for which the reported detail was normal.
- *Chemical explanations the item did not consider.* For the mixed yield bases, product volatility (a low-boiling product makes an isolated yield impractical). For the black solid, metallic Ni⁰ from decomposition. For homo-coupling, near-equimolar reagents with yields up to 98% leave little room for by-product.
- *Short-lived intermediates.* Isolating the proposed intermediates would be hard if the cycle turns over, because isolable intermediates would imply a stalled cycle.
- *Background knowledge.* One comment drew on knowledge of the field that is not in the paper.
- *Research value.* The β-H elimination item was described as worth deeper study, and its later development in ligand research was noted.
