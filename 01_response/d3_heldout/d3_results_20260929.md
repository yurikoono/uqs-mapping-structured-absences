as follows: the SRG/CLA block names an EP-NNx class, **and** contains either a quotation from the paper (≥ 15 characters in
quotation marks) or a reference to a location in the paper (page, table, scheme, figure, entry, example, SI, reference, or
named section). **UQS item IDs (A-/B-/U-n) are not counted**, because they point to UQS output, not to the paper.

**Comparison with the refinement set (Methods 6).** v17 refinement set: 4 misreports in 78 key_quote anchors. Kumada/Murai v18.4:
0 in 33. Held-out v18.4: 3 distinct misreports in 4 papers.
**The v18.4 result on the development set does not generalise.** All three misreports are *substitutions or insertions of a content
word that fits the paper's context*: Ni-phosphine for Heck-type in a Ni-catalysis paper; anti-cancer (taken from the ref. 1 title) for drugs;
"concerted" added to an electrocyclization. Each looks plausible to a reader and cannot be caught without checking the source.
The meaning-preserving compression class (9 rows) is a separate quote-discipline issue: wording is altered inside quotation marks.

**Screening consistency (Fable 5.1 review F8).** Criterion 4 was applied inconsistently. Nine eligible papers use Heck coupling only
as a step (a fluorescent probe, an antifungal series, a total synthesis, porphyrins, and multi-coupling catalyst papers), while
Heck–Cassar–Sonogashira papers were excluded. None of the four drawn papers is affected (each has a Heck-type reaction as its subject).
The inconsistency is disclosed rather than re-drawn. Only 38 of the 76 date-eligible records have a Crossref abstract.
