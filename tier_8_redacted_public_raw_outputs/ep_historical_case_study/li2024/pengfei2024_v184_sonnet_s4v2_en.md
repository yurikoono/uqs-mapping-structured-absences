---

### §A Three-tier classification of EP filling difficulty

**Tier 1 (clearly trackable in the literature):** EP-03a, EP-04a, EP-07a
- EP-03a: In-situ EPR/UV-Vis spectroelectrochemical detection of Ni(I) in electroreductive catalysis is a concrete, named experiment pursued as an independent research objective; Ni(I) d⁹ EPR papers in Ni cross-coupling are identifiable by targeted search.
- EP-04a: Radical-clock rearranged:unrearranged ratio quantification and DLPNO-CCSD(T) benchmarking of Ni RE barriers are both subjects of independent research programs; radical-clock mechanistic papers in Ni eXEC appear as stand-alone contributions.
- EP-07a: Systematic ligand/solvent comparison (L2/DMF vs. L11/DMA) for Ni-electrocatalysed eXEC of sterically demanding substrates is the type of focused mechanistic study that competing groups publish independently.

**Tier 2 (indirectly trackable):** EP-01a, EP-05a, EP-06a, EP-08a, EP-09a
- EP-01a: DFT methodology disclosure and unified reference electrode reporting are addressed within expanded mechanistic papers or Full Paper SI, not as primary stand-alone publications; self-filling in a sequel is the most likely path.
- EP-05a: Quantitative selectivity rate-law derivation from existing Fig. 4b data requires no new experiments but does not meet novelty standards for independent publication under current conventions.
- EP-06a: Anodic half-reaction identification (ICP-OES, Faradaic efficiency) is more commonly a subsection of an expanded electrochemical synthesis paper.
- EP-08a: Complete mass balance with GC-yield internal standard is typically embedded in a Full Paper SI extension of a communication.
- EP-09a: Head-to-head comparison with Lin 2022 and Gong 2011/2013 on shared substrates is most likely to appear in a review, not as primary research.

**Tier 3 (filling may not be found):** none

**Evaluation of hierarchy–time correspondence:**
Partially supported. VQ-2 field-norm EPs (EP-01a, EP-06a conceptual) are filled by papers predating Li 2024 by 24–36 years, consistent with the expectation that basic methodological standards are established early. VQ-3 mechanistic EPs are addressed only partially by contemporaneous 2022 papers, and EP-03a (the highest mechanistic priority) remains unfilled. **Notable patterns:**

- **Cross-field filling (historical):** EP-06a — Nédélec 1988 originates in organic electrochemistry and fills the anodic chemistry concept before Ni-electrocatalysis was a distinct subfield.
- **VQ-hierarchy inversion:** EP-08a (VQ-4, RI-08) partially filled earlier than EP-03a (VQ-3, RI-03). The publication incentive structure rewards scope papers incorporating mass balance by convention, while targeted Ni(I) EPR spectroelectrochemistry papers require specific infrastructure and have limited stand-alone publication outlets.

### §E Heuristic significance of unfilled EPs

**EP-03a [Direct-observation / state-assignment pack]:**
* **Reason for non-filling**: Technical difficulty + misaligned incentive structure. In-situ EPR spectroelectrochemistry on Ni(I)/L2/DMF/NaI under 5 mA Fe-anode conditions requires purpose-built thin-layer cells compatible with EPR cavity geometry; the undivided-cell format of Li 2024 is incompatible with standard EPR cells. A stand-alone Ni(I) EPR paper without new synthetic scope has limited outlets under novelty-demanding journal standards.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes. EP-03a exposes a field-wide structural absence: Ni(I) is invoked as the central active species in every major eXEC paper in this subfield (Li 2024, Lin 2022, Sevov 2020/2022, Truesdell 2020), yet no paper in the eXEC subfield has directly observed Ni(I) under turnover conditions as of the knowledge cutoff. UQS documents a community-level gap maintained across ≥4 years of active research — the foundational mechanistic proposal rests on an unobserved intermediate across multiple groups' publications.
* **Future filling feasibility**: Medium. Modern benchtop EPR (e.g., Bruker MicroESR) with purpose-built spectroelectrochemical cells makes this technically accessible within 1–2 months of cell development. UV-Vis spectroelectrochemistry (lower infrastructure barrier) could provide a preliminary stepping-stone filling.

---

**EP-05a [Quantitative-ranking substantiation pack] — unfilled as independent paper:**
* **Reason for non-filling**: Misaligned incentive structure. All data required to fill EP-05a are present in Li 2024 (Fig. 4b slopes, Fig. 4e DFT barriers, Entry 1 distribution 85:17:13). Deriving k₁/k₂ and comparing predicted to observed homocoupling yields requires only kinetic modelling — no new experiments. Such a paper does not meet novelty standards for primary research journals. The deposited kinetic simulation code may implement a descriptive rather than predictive model.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes. EP-05a documents a recurring pattern in selectivity-claiming electrochemical synthesis papers: selectivity numbers are reported as performance metrics, but the mechanism's quantitative predictive validity is never tested against those numbers. The 85:17:13 product distribution is simultaneously Li 2024's central selectivity evidence and the strongest unasked challenge to the proposed selectivity model — a self-contradictory absence that the field systematically does not flag.
* **Future filling feasibility**: High. All data are in hand; self-filling via an expanded kinetic analysis in a Full Paper sequel requires weeks of computational work at most.

---

**EP-09a [Benchmark comparison pack]:**
* **Reason for non-filling**: Misaligned incentive structure (primary). Running Li 2024, Lin 2022, and Gong 2011/2013 on ≥3 shared substrates requires significant experimental effort with no new chemistry discovered; the result does not meet novelty standards for primary research journals. The "first general eXEC" claim is validated in Li 2024 by definitional exclusion of prior art rather than by experimental comparison on shared substrates — a field-wide convention maintained by publication incentives that privilege scope demonstrations.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes. EP-09a identifies a structural gap in how "first general" methodology claims are validated across the eXEC field. UQS makes visible that the competitive claim rests on definitional framing rather than experimental demonstration — a systematic evidence gap maintained across multiple competing groups' papers in this subfield.
* **Future filling feasibility**: Medium. Technically straightforward (shared substrates are commercially available; both protocols are published). Primary obstacle is incentive structure. Most likely filling mechanism: a review or perspective with a methods comparison table (excluded from formal filling status per Stage 4 guidelines unless original comparative experimental data are included).
