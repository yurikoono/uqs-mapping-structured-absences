# EP-Filling Selection Map

## Eno, Lu, and Morken 2016 (Journal of the American Chemical Society, communication)

---

### §A Three-tier classification of EP filling difficulty

**Tier 1 (clearly trackable in the literature):** EP-04a, EP-03a, EP-06a, EP-07a

- **EP-04a**: EPR and rate-matched clock experiments distinguishing Ni(I)/Ni(III) from Ni(0)/Ni(II) were published as primary mechanistic contributions driven by the community-wide Ni mechanism debate 2012–2022.
- **EP-03a**: Stoichiometric Ni(II) complex synthesis (XRD/EPR/CV) and Grignard speciation studies appear as independent primary papers; the three EP-03a sub-components each have a distinct independent literature.
- **EP-06a**: DFT TS calculations for Ni(0)/PyBox C–O oxidative addition and the Jarvo group's experimental Ni(0)/Ni(II) two-electron C–O coupling precedent (molken2016 refs 9a–9d) directly address the analogy-validation requirement.
- **EP-07a**: Mass-balance and bis-coupling competition tests are straightforward GC experiments expected in a full Article companion from the original group.

**Tier 2 (indirectly trackable):** EP-08a, EP-01a, EP-05a

- **EP-08a**: SFC column documentation + replicate SD are addressed in full Article companions to communications and method-validation contexts, not as stand-alone primary papers.
- **EP-01a**: Ligand-ranking confirmation under corrected deprotection protocol and background er reporting are characteristically provided in full Articles or errata.
- **EP-05a**: Eyring analysis for enantioselective Ni desymmetrizations and constrained-substrate synthesis for reactive-conformation discrimination are embedded in comprehensive mechanistic papers rather than stand-alone communications.

**Tier 3 (filling may not be found):** EP-09a

- **EP-09a**: Direct experimental comparison of the molken2016 system vs. Jarvo/Fu/Nakamura prior-method entries on a common substrate satisfies no novelty criterion; misaligned incentive structure prevents independent publication.

### §D Correspondence between VQ hierarchy and filling time order

**Filling time axis:**

- VQ-2 (EP-06a, cycle-level): → 2012, Jarvo et al. [Cross-field filling; pre-dates 2016 paper]
- VQ-2 (EP-08a, EP-01a, EP-07a): → 2017–2020, Morken group (tentative_citation) [Self-filling; unconfirmed]
- VQ-3 (EP-04a): → 2016, Diao et al. [Technology-driven; EPR methodology]
- VQ-3 (EP-03a, transmetalating species): → 2016, Haas/Knochel [Cross-field; Grignard speciation community]
- VQ-3 (EP-03a, reductive elimination): → 2016–2020, Chierchia or equivalent [Technology-driven; stoichiometric Ni(II)]
- VQ-3 (EP-05a, EP-06a geometry): → 2016–2022, DFT papers [Technology-driven; computational chemistry]
- VQ-4 (EP-09a): → unfilled

**Evaluation of hierarchy–time correspondence:**

Qualified support. VQ-2 cycle-level analogy (EP-06a) was pre-filled by the Jarvo group before molken2016 — zero-delay, driven by prior art structure. VQ-3 mechanistic EPs were addressed concurrently with the paper's publication, driven by the broader Ni mechanism debate. Notable inversion: the lower-difficulty VQ-2 analytical EPs (EP-08a, EP-01a) are less confirmed as filled than VQ-3 mechanistic EPs, because they depend on self-filling in a full Article that may never have been published.

**Notable patterns:**

- **Technology-driven filling** dominates VQ-3: EPR (Diao), DFT TS (Taylor), stoichiometric Ni(II) (Chierchia) emerged from the community-wide Ni mechanism debate.
- **Cross-field filling**: Jarvo group (EP-06a cycle level) and Knochel Grignard speciation (EP-03a transmetalating species) fill from adjacent programs independently of molken2016.
- **Self-filling (anticipated, unconfirmed)**: EP-08a and EP-01a depend on a Morken group full Article; communication → full Article transition failure would permanently open these analytical gaps.
- **Long-delay filling (potential)**: EP-07a — trivially feasible (crude GC) but low novelty incentive as a standalone contribution.
- **Unfilled**: EP-09a.

### §E Heuristic significance of unfilled EPs

**EP-09a [Internal benchmark pack]:**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Misaligned incentive structure. Direct comparison of molken2016 vs. Jarvo/Fu/Nakamura on a common substrate satisfies no novelty criterion; no group publishes a paper whose primary contribution is reproducing a competitor's er result for benchmarking. Reviews discuss methods qualitatively but are not filling papers.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes. EP-09a is a question the field has systematically avoided: direct experimental benchmarking of radical-based enantioconvergent vs. two-electron Ni(0)/Ni(II) desymmetrization on common substrates has not appeared as a primary-literature contribution; relative merits rest on narrative positioning rather than independent experimental evidence.
* **Future filling feasibility**: Medium — technically trivial; publishing barrier remains under novelty conventions; a commissioned multi-group Perspective with direct comparative tables would be the most tractable vehicle.

---

**EP-08a [Replication / uncertainty pack] — conditionally unfilled (self-filling unconfirmed):**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Reporting-format issue (communication → full Article transition failure). SFC documentation and duplicate SD gaps were absorbed as era-norm-tolerated omissions; if no full Article companion expanded SI documentation, the 17-entry er dataset lacks independent verification infrastructure.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes (conditional). If confirmed unfilled, molken2016 is a case study in the analytical-integrity gap of the communication-only pathway: the paper's entire primary evidence base remains unverifiable.
* **Future filling feasibility**: High — SFC records presumably exist in Morken group notebooks; retroactive publication in a data paper or review would resolve EP-08a without new experiments.

**VQ-hierarchy inversion diagnosis:**

Partial inversion confirmed: EP-08a (VQ-2, RI-01/RI-08) and EP-09a (VQ-4, RI-09) — lowest-VQ reporting and highest-VQ benchmark requirements — are the least confirmed as filled, while mid-VQ mechanistic EPs (EP-04a, EP-03a, EP-06a) attracted independent community investment. Pattern: low-VQ reporting unfilled due to format absorption; high-VQ benchmark unfilled due to incentive structure; mid-VQ mechanism indirectly filled by community debate. This is direct evidence that field format and incentive structures maintain structural absences at precisely the VQ boundaries requiring the least scientific novelty to fill.
