### §A Three-tier classification of EP filling difficulty

**Tier 1 (clearly trackable in the literature):** EP-03a, EP-04a, EP-08a, EP-09a
- **EP-03a**: Kinetic characterization of L₂Ni(Ar)(alkyl) RE/β-HE competition is the main subject of organometallic mechanism papers; independent synthesis, NMR, and thermal decay measurement are identifiable stand-alone research.

**Tier 2 (indirectly trackable):** EP-01a, EP-05a, EP-07a

- **EP-07a**: ArF yield/conditions may exist as a table entry in [10a] or [10d]; archival submission dates are retrievable from journal mastheads.

**Tier 3 (filling may not be found):** none — all EPs are associated with RI codes that make Tier 1 or Tier 2 plausible; tamao2002 is itself a retrospective mini-review so primary papers already exist.

### §D Correspondence between VQ hierarchy and filling time order

**Filling time axis:**

- VQ-2 / EP-05a → 1972 (Tamao, Kiso, Sumitani, Kumada JACS) — self-filling, concurrent with discovery.
- VQ-2 / EP-08a (Pd baseline) → 1976 (Negishi, Baba) — external, 4 yr.
- VQ-2 / EP-01a → 1976 (Tamao et al. BCSJ) — self-filling, 4 yr.
- VQ-3 / EP-04a SRG-3 (prototype) → 1975 (Yamamura et al.) — content-determination; fillable by reading [11a].
- VQ-3 / EP-04a SRG-1 (radical-clock OA) → 1979 (Tsou, Kochi) — external technology-driven, 7 yr.
- VQ-3 / EP-03a (RE/β-HE, Pd analogy) → 1980 (Gillie, Stille) — cross-field, 8 yr.
- VQ-4 / EP-09a (Ni/Pd benchmark) → 1987 (Minato, Suzuki, Tamao Kohei) — self-filling, 15 yr.
- VQ-3 / EP-03a (direct Ni kinetics) and EP-05a (isomerization mechanism): **unidentified**.

**Evaluation of hierarchy–time correspondence:** Data weakly support the hypothesis: VQ-2 fillings clustered at 0–4 years; VQ-4 required 15 years. However, VQ-3 shows high internal heterogeneity: the historiometric prototype EP (SRG-3) was nominally fillable by 1975 (3 years), while chemical-mechanistic VQ-3 EPs (EP-03a, EP-05a mechanism) remain partly or fully unidentified. Historiometric VQ-3 items require only literature retrieval; chemical-mechanistic VQ-3 items required new experimental infrastructure that the community deprioritized after the field shifted to Pd.

**Notable patterns:**
- **Self-filling** (dominant): Papers 1, 2, 3 are all Kumada/Tamao group self-fillings — primary mode for VQ-2 and VQ-4.
- **Cross-field filling** (EP-03a): Gillie–Stille 1980 Pd kinetics fills EP-03a by analogy; direct Ni filling absent.
- **Long-delay filling** (EP-07a CLA-1): ArF mechanistic data arrived ~35 years later via the separate Ni C–F activation field.
- **Comparison with author retrospective**: Tamao Kohei 2002 presents Scheme 1 and features 1–10 as settled without acknowledging EP-03a, EP-05a (dmpe reversal), or CLA-1 (ArF) as open — insider invisibility of VQ-3 mechanistic EPs is direct evidence of structural absences that UQS is designed to surface. EP-09a (Ni > Pd, feature 9) is partially visible but without framing the absence of quantitative rate comparison.

### §E Heuristic significance of unfilled EPs

**EP-03a [Direct Ni(Ar)(alkyl)(dppp) kinetics under catalytic conditions — SRG-1]:**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Technical difficulty + misaligned incentive structure. Synthesizing L₂Ni(Ph)(n-Bu)(dppp) at −78 °C and measuring k(RE)/k(β-HE) is experimentally demanding; the mid-1970s field shift to Pd removed the incentive for Ni intermediate kinetics, and the Yamamoto analogy was accepted as sufficient mechanistic grounding.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes. The k(RE)/k(β-HE) ratio for Ni(dppp) under catalytic conditions has never been measured — the kinetic crux of the "most significant feature" claim (feature 4, eq. 2) persists >50 years unfilled; the gap is invisible to the field because the selectivity works in practice.
* **Future filling feasibility**: High. Resurgent Ni catalysis (Ni(I)/Ni(III) radical pathways, ~2010+) plus modern cryo-NMR and stopped-flow methods make this feasible and timely.

---

**EP-05a [Mechanistic attribution of sec-alkyl isomerization — isotope-labeling crossover]:**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Field convention + misaligned incentive structure. Isomerization was documented in 1972–1973 ([13a,b]) as a selectivity observation. After Pd displaced Ni, the Ni(dppp/dmpe) isomerization mechanism became an orphaned question; the α-¹³C labeling experiment was feasible post-1975 but found no champion.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes. The dmpe direction reversal (stronger σ-donor dmpe disfavors primary product, opposite to chain-walking prediction) is one of the structurally sharpest UQS findings: an anomalous data point from 1972 absorbed into "characteristic features" for >50 years without mechanistic attribution.
* **Future filling feasibility**: High. α-¹³C-sec-butylMgCl from commercial ¹³CH₃I; ¹H-decoupled ¹³C NMR resolves C-1 vs. C-2 labeling. Executable in any modern organometallic laboratory.

---

**EP-07a [CLA-1: quantitative yield and conditions for ArF cross-coupling, pre-2001]:**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Reporting-format issue + lack of interest. The ArF claim was parenthetical without conditions or citation; Ni C–F activation research did not emerge until the late 2000s, by which time the Kumada-era observation was historical.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes. A 50-year-old scope-boundary anomaly that was never quantitatively published cannot anchor prior art for the Ni C–F activation field even though it predates the field by decades — a field-convention artifact.
* **Future filling feasibility**: Medium. Path 1: retrieve from [10a] or [10d] tables (library task, uncertain). Path 2: repeat under Ni(dppp)Cl₂ conditions (straightforward, limited novelty in 2026).
