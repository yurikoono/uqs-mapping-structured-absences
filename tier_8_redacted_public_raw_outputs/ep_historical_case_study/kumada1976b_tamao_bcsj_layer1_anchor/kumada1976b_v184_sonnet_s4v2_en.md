### §A Three-tier classification of EP filling difficulty

**Tier 1 (clearly trackable in the literature):** EP-03a, EP-04a, EP-07a, EP-02a

- **EP-03a** [Direct-observation / state-assignment pack]: The demand for ³¹P NMR and UV-vis characterization of nickel-phosphine intermediates under catalytic conditions is exactly the subject of numerous independent organonickel mechanistic papers published from 1977 onward; the yellow diorganonickel species from [Ni(dppp)Cl₂] + alkyl Grignard reagents is concrete enough to be hit by a literature search for "dialkylnickel diphosphine spectroscopic characterization."
- **EP-04a** [Discrimination / replication pack]: Crossover experiments designed to distinguish Scheme 1 (diorganonickel intermediate) from the Felkin-Corriu Ni(0) oxidative-addition mechanism constitute an independent experimental program that was addressed as a primary research question in the mechanistic literature of the late 1970s and early 1980s.
- **EP-07a** [Boundary-test pack]: The use of β-hydride-free neopentyl or neophyl alkyl Grignard reagents to probe the mechanistic origin of the alkyl Grignard + dihaloethylene failure mode is a concrete, independently executable experiment whose results would have been published as part of the Ni-cross-coupling mechanistic literature.
- **EP-02a** [Identity / purity substantiation pack]: The isolation of butylbenzene from PhF + n-BuMgBr with Ni-phosphine catalysis is a specific, verifiable experiment; the question of whether C–F oxidative addition occurs with Ni(0) species was actively pursued by inorganic/organometallic chemists independently of the Kumada group.

**Tier 2 (indirectly trackable):** EP-05a, EP-08a, EP-01a, EP-09a

- **EP-05a** [Quantitative-ranking substantiation pack]: The documentation of ¹H NMR J-coupling constants for (E)- and (Z)-stilbene, and systematic GC headspace analysis for β-elimination olefin by-products, is addressed indirectly in stereospecificity studies and in the Tamao group's own follow-up communications, but not typically as the sole focus of an independent paper; it is more often embedded in broader mechanistic or scope papers.
- **EP-08a** [Replication / uncertainty pack]: Replicate GLPC measurements with standard deviations for the Table 1 reference reaction and representative scope entries are not the subject of an independent paper; this kind of analytical-method validation is embedded in Full Papers or methods-validation reports rather than appearing as the primary subject of an independent research article.
- **EP-01a** [Reporting-coherence pack]: FID response factor documentation, mass balance verification, and reconciliation of yield denominators for the ~60-entry scope are reporting-coherence issues that are not the independent subject of a published paper; they are partially addressed in the Tamao group's Full Papers (planned "Part II" sequels) or embedded in the Organic Syntheses procedure for one substrate (1,2-dichlorobenzene + n-BuMgBr), but not as a standalone filling paper.
- **EP-09a** [Internal benchmark pack]: Direct substrate comparison of the Ni-phosphine system against organocuprate protocols on matched substrates is addressed in review articles and in the secondary cross-coupling literature, but not always as the primary focus of an independent research paper dedicated to head-to-head comparison; the closest filling candidates are methodology comparison papers.

**Tier 3 (filling may not be found):** EP-06a

- **EP-06a** [Analogy-validation pack]: The comparison of the Mg-specific transmetalation effect in the Ni system with the Mg-dependent transmetalation in Tamura-Kochi Fe/Cu Grignard coupling is an analogy-validation demand that arises from an unpublished observation (ref. 48); filling papers that specifically address the Mg vs. Li metal identity effect at the transmetalation step of a Ni(II)-phosphine cycle as a primary research question are not expected to exist as independent papers, because the question is embedded in the broader mechanistic literature rather than driving an independent publication.
