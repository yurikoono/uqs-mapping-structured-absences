**Tier 1 (clearly trackable in the literature):** EP-02a, EP-03a, EP-06a, EP-09a

- **EP-02a** [Identity / purity substantiation pack]: UV-vis or ESI-MS characterisation of Ni(0) versus Ni(II) activation products of NiCp-phosphine precatalysts is a well-defined experimental target that has been published as the primary subject of independent mechanistic organometallic papers. Characterisation of the active Ni species in cross-coupling precatalyst activation is a concrete, trackable experimental question.
- **EP-03a** [Direct-observation / state-assignment pack]: Initial-rate kinetics and Hammett analysis of Ni-catalysed Kumada coupling are concrete experimental designs that generate independent publications; the rate-limiting step question for Ni/diphosphine cross-coupling has been addressed as the main subject of multiple mechanistic papers.
- **EP-06a** [Analogy-validation pack]: EPR monitoring of Ni catalysts during cross-coupling reactions and radical clock experiments for mechanistic pathway discrimination have been published as independent mechanistic studies, especially after 2011 when Ni radical manifold work became a major research theme.
- **EP-09a** [Internal benchmark pack]: Head-to-head comparison of Ni/calixdiphosphine versus Ni/dppp at matched conditions is a concrete single-experiment EP that a follow-up paper from the Matt group or a comparative review would naturally supply.

**Tier 2 (indirectly trackable):** EP-01a, EP-04a, EP-05a, EP-08a

- **EP-01a** [Reporting-coherence pack]: Resolution of the internal ³¹P NMR discrepancy (Δδ = 9.8 ppm, ΔJ = 6.2 Hz between main text and Experimental) requires access to the raw FID or a corrected re-characterisation that is unlikely to appear as the main subject of an independent paper; it would appear as a correction notice or in a follow-up full paper from the same group.
- **EP-04a** [Discrimination / replication pack]: Explicit commentary on the ortho/meta-bromotoluene regiochemical insensitivity requires a mechanistic discussion that is most likely embedded within a broader substrate-scope or mechanistic paper rather than standing alone.
- **EP-05a** [Quantitative-ranking substantiation pack]: A Hammett σ plot using the Table 3 ArBr data, or steric tolerance boundary testing with 2,6-dimethylbromobenzene, could appear in a methodology paper but is more likely embedded in a review or a follow-up broad-scope study.
- **EP-08a** [Replication / uncertainty pack]: Isolated yields and per-substrate homocoupling data for the headline loading entries are most likely to appear in a subsequent Full Paper from the Matt group expanding the substrate scope, not as an independent replication study.

**Tier 3 (filling may not be found):** EP-07a, EP-10a

- **EP-07a** [Boundary-test pack]: Testing [NiCl₂(dppp)] with bromomesitylene and testing 2,6-dimethylbromobenzene to locate the steric tolerance limit are negative-result boundary experiments with low novelty for independent publication under 2009–2020 publication norms; they would more likely appear as supplementary entries in a broader scope paper.
- **EP-10a** [Kinetic verification pack, provisional]: Systematic rate-law determination by variation of [Ni], [PhMgBr], and [ArBr] for this specific calixdiphosphine/Ni system is technically feasible but lacks the novelty premium required for independent publication; such detailed kinetics for a single ligand-metal combination would appear embedded in a comprehensive mechanistic study rather than as a standalone paper.

**Evaluation of hierarchy–time correspondence:**

The data are too sparse and too low-confidence for a robust test of the hierarchy–time hypothesis. The tentative filling time axis shows that VQ-3 requirements (EP-06a, ~2013–2014) were addressed before VQ-4 requirements (EP-05a, EP-07a, still unidentified), which is consistent with the hypothesis. However, VQ-2 requirements (EP-02a, EP-08a, EP-09a) remain largely unfilled at the direct experimental level, creating an apparent inversion: higher VQ levels (VQ-3 via EP-06a) were partially addressed by the general field before lower VQ levels (VQ-2 via EP-09a) were addressed for this specific system. This inversion reflects the field's incentive structure rather than logical necessity: the mechanistic paradigm question (Ni(I)/Ni(III) vs. Ni(0)/Ni(II)) attracted independent research investment, while the specific benchmark comparison for the calixdiphosphine system did not.

**Notable patterns:**

*Paradigm-shift filling (EP-06a):* The most consequential filling is paradigm-shift rather than direct: the Ni(I)/Ni(III) radical mechanism literature (2012–2020) reframes the entire mechanistic hypothesis of Monnereau 2009 without directly studying the calixdiphosphine/Ni system. *Lack of self-filling:* No self-filling paper from the Sémeril/Matt group has been identified with confidence. The Matt group has published subsequent calixarene-related work, but a targeted follow-up to Monnereau 2009 that resolves EP-08a (isolated yields) or EP-09a (extended dppp comparison) was not confirmed. *Long-delay filling (EP-02a, EP-03a, EP-09a):* More than 15 years after publication, EP-02a, EP-09a, and EP-10a remain unfilled for this specific system. This qualifies as long-delay non-filling (>15 years) for a set of EPs that the paper's own mechanistic claims require.

*Misaligned incentive structure:* EP-07a (boundary test of steric scope), EP-04a (regiochemical discrimination), and EP-10a (rate-law determination for a single ligand/metal combination) are unfilled due to misaligned publication incentives: these experiments are technically feasible but lack the novelty premium required for independent publication. ### §E Heuristic significance of unfilled EPs

EP-01a [Reporting-coherence pack]:

* **Reason for non-filling**: Reporting-format issue. The internal ³¹P NMR discrepancy (Δδ_B = 9.8 ppm, ΔJ = 6.2 Hz between main text and Experimental section at nominally identical temperatures) can only be resolved by the original authors or by a group re-characterising complex 2. * **Heuristic significance**: Yes. Any group attempting to reproduce or extend the bite-angle-dynamics claim must resolve whether the low-temperature ³¹P NMR values are those in the main text or those in the Experimental. The field has implicitly accepted the discrepancy without resolution.
* **Future filling feasibility**: High — re-recording ³¹P NMR of complex 2 at 193 K at 600 MHz is technically straightforward if the compound is available.

* **Reason for non-filling**: Technical difficulty (requires synthesis and re-activation of complex 2 under controlled conditions) combined with lack of interest (the cross-coupling community moved from Ni(0)/Ni(II) mechanistic focus to Ni(I)/Ni(III) radical mechanisms after 2012, reducing incentive to characterise the Ni(0) activation product of this specific system).

* **Future filling feasibility**: High — UV-vis and ESI-MS of the activation product is technically routine and could be performed by any group with access to the calixdiphosphine ligand.

**VQ-hierarchy inversion diagnosis:**

A clear inversion between the EP-filling order dictated by scientific logic (low-VQ → high-VQ) and the actual filling order dictated by publication incentives (high-VQ → low-VQ) is evident in this case. The VQ-3/VQ-4 level EP (EP-06a, Ni radical mechanism) received indirect filling through the general Ni mechanistic literature (~2013–2014) before VQ-2 level EPs (EP-08a isolated yields, EP-09a extended benchmark comparison) received any filling at all. The inversion is structurally consistent with the general pattern identified in §E of the prompt: high-VQ mechanistic EPs attract independent research investment (because they require new methodology and generate paradigm-level findings), while low-VQ verification EPs (isolated yields, extended benchmark comparisons, per-substrate homocoupling) are systematically avoided because they lack the novelty premium required for independent publication. The calixdiphosphine/Ni Kumada system exemplifies this inversion: the performance claim (VQ-2) is more widely cited than the mechanistic claim (VQ-3), but the VQ-3 mechanistic domain received more independent follow-up (via the Ni radical mechanism literature) than the VQ-2 verification domain (which received none at the direct experimental level).
