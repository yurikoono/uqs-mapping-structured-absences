### §A Three-tier classification of EP filling difficulty

**Tier 1 (clearly trackable in the literature):** EP-03a, EP-04a, EP-06a, EP-07a

— **EP-03a** [Direct-observation / state-assignment pack]: Synthesis and characterization of [dpe-Ni(R)(X)] and [dpe-Ni(R)(R')] intermediates is a concrete organometallic objective that resulted in independent papers by the Kumada group and others during the 1970s; the required experiment (isolate intermediate, verify reductive elimination) is the main subject of follow-up full papers.

— **EP-04a** [Discrimination / replication pack]: The demand for explicit cross:homo product ratios with stated detection limits for a specific reaction entry (EtMgBr/PhCl or n-BuMgBr/PhCl) is sufficiently concrete that the Tamao–Kumada 1977 full paper and subsequent mechanistic papers address this directly.

— **EP-06a** [Analogy-validation pack]: Characterization of the black precipitate and validation of the homogeneous vs. heterogeneous catalyst question is a specific physical chemistry experiment that was addressed in the organometallic mechanism literature of the 1970s–1980s.

— **EP-07a** [Boundary-test pack]: Whether sp³ halides can be used with Ni-dpe catalysis or require modified conditions is a concrete synthetic question that generated a substantial literature; the identification of Ni(0)/Ni(II) cycles and the subsequent development of sp³-compatible Ni catalysis (and its failures) constitutes direct filling activity.

**Tier 2 (indirectly trackable):** EP-05a, EP-08a, EP-09a

— **EP-05a** [Quantitative-ranking substantiation pack]: The re-determination of cis:trans stilbene ratios with stated analytical precision was not the subject of any independent paper; stereochemical studies of the Kumada coupling are embedded within full papers and mechanistic studies, making this a Tier 2 item traceable in the context of broader mechanistic investigations.

— **EP-08a** [Replication / uncertainty pack]: Replicate yield determination with stated standard deviations for representative Table I entries is covered in the 1977 full paper context, but as part of a broader systematic scope expansion rather than as a dedicated replication study; the filling is indirect.

— **EP-09a** [Internal benchmark pack]: Head-to-head comparison of [NiCl₂(dpe)] vs. Tamura-Kochi Fe catalyst and vs. Ni(acac)₂ (Corriu-Masse) on a shared substrate — such a direct comparison was not the main subject of any single independent paper in the 1972–1980 period; the comparative data accumulated across multiple method papers and reviews, making this Tier 2.

**Tier 3 (filling may not be found):** EP-01a

— **EP-01a** [Reporting-coherence pack]: The revision of Table I footnotes to specify yield basis per entry, internal standard identity, and response factors for glpc quantification is an RI-01/RI-08 item that is automatically resolved in the communication → Full Paper transition without constituting a separately publishable finding; no independent paper fills this EP because the resolution is structural (format upgrade), not experimental.

**EP-04a [Discrimination / replication pack] (Tier 1)**

* **RI to be filled**: RI-04 [Unreplicated discriminator] (primary — the self-contradictory absence of homo-coupling byproduct, CLA-1)
* **Filling Onset**: Tamao, Sumitani, Hayashi, and Kumada, *Bull. Chem. Soc. Jpn.* **49**, 1958 (1976) [tentative_citation] — The full paper is expected to address the selectivity question more explicitly, including some discussion of byproduct distribution if any homo-coupling products were observed.
* **Filling Establishment**: The filling of this EP is most directly accomplished by mechanistic studies in the 1974–1982 period that established the Ni(0)/Ni(II) catalytic cycle as the operative mechanism and determined that the homo-coupling byproduct (R–R from eq 3) arises at sub-catalytic levels because eq 3 represents an initiation step rather than a propagation step. The key mechanistic insight — that [L₂NiR₂] reacts with R'X to generate R–R only in the initiation phase, and that the catalytic cycle thereafter proceeds via [L₂Ni(0)] ⇌ [L₂Ni(II)(R)(X)] without further R–R generation — was developed in:

  **Hayashi, Tajika, Tamao, and Kumada**, *J. Am. Chem. Soc.* **98**, 3718 (1976) [tentative_citation: Hayashi and Kumada published in JACS in the mid-1970s on Ni-catalyzed cross-coupling stereochemistry and mechanism; the specific paper reference requires verification] — provides mechanistic evidence relevant to the cycle and byproduct question.

  The clearer direct filling is the paradigm-shift filling described below.

* **Nature of filling**: Paradigm-shift — the revised understanding of the catalytic cycle (Ni(0)/Ni(II) rather than Ni(II)/Ni(II)) changes the framing of the EP: in the Ni(0)/Ni(II) cycle, the oxidative addition of R'X occurs to Ni(0), and the homo-coupling byproduct (R–R) arises from the initiation step (reductive elimination from [L₂NiR₂]) only once per catalyst loading rather than per turnover. This makes the expected homo-coupling yield equal to the catalyst loading × (Grignard equivalents consumed in initiation), i.e., 1–3 mol% relative to product — below the glpc detection threshold used in 1972. The paradigm shift resolves the self-contradictory absence by revealing that eq 3 describes the catalyst activation step, not a propagation step, so R–R is generated sub-stoichiometrically with respect to substrate.
* **Confidence**: Medium — the mechanistic paradigm shift from Ni(II)/Ni(II) to Ni(0)/Ni(II) is well-established in the cross-coupling literature and resolves the CLA-1 self-contradictory absence; the specific papers achieving this shift in the 1974–1982 period are known but individual citation details require verification.
* **Notes**: The CLA-1 item (U-1 + B-6) is filled by the mechanistic paradigm shift rather than by a dedicated selectivity measurement paper. The EP demanded [REDACTED: substantial verbatim quotation; see Tier 9] — this was not done as an independent experiment, but the mechanistic revision rendered the EP moot for the high-severity item (the R–R byproduct level is now understood to be at catalyst loading levels, i.e., ≪1% product).

### §D Correspondence between VQ hierarchy and filling time order

**Filling time axis:**

- VQ-2 requirement [RI-01 / RI-07 — reporting coherence and scope boundary]:
  - EP-01a → 1976 [Tamao–Kumada full paper, tentative] — partial, self-filling (automatic-resolution pattern via communication → Full Paper)
  - EP-07a → 1972 (onset, Corriu-Masse); 1976 (establishment, Tamao–Kumada full paper) — complete filling of synthetic scope boundary; partial self-filling

- VQ-3 requirement [RI-03 / RI-06 — mechanism and intermediate characterization]:
  - EP-03a → 1976 (onset, Kumada group intermediate papers); 1980 (establishment, Yamamoto group) — self-filling + cross-field filling (Yamamoto group at Osaka)
  - EP-04a → 1976 (paradigm-shift onset, Hayashi-Kumada JACS) — paradigm-shift filling
  - EP-05a → 1976 (partial, Hayashi-Kumada mechanistic study) — partial self-filling
  - EP-06a → 1976 (paradigm-shift onset); 1980 (establishment) — paradigm-shift filling + self-filling

- VQ-4 requirement [RI-08 / RI-09 — replication and external benchmarking]:
  - EP-08a → 1978 (partial, Org. Synth. procedure, tentative) — partial filling via format channel
  - EP-09a → 1972 (concurrent parallel, Corriu-Masse); 1982 (comparative context, Negishi review) — indirect filling only; no primary-paper direct benchmark identified

**Evaluation of hierarchy–time correspondence:**

The data broadly support the hierarchy–time hypothesis, but with a structurally interesting exception. The VQ-2-level EPs (EP-01a, EP-07a) have the shortest time to filling: EP-07a begins filling in 1972 (the same year as the original paper, via the concurrent Corriu-Masse communication) and is substantially complete by 1976. The VQ-3-level EPs (EP-03a, EP-04a, EP-06a) require the longest time to decisive filling: 4–8 years (1972→1976–1980), reflecting the true scientific difficulty of establishing the catalytic mechanism and characterizing the intermediates. The VQ-4-level EPs (EP-08a, EP-09a) show an anomalous pattern: they have not achieved decisive filling even by 2026, but this is not because they are scientifically difficult (they are not) — it is because the publication incentive structure does not reward replicate-yield papers or dedicated head-to-head comparison papers as independent contributions. This constitutes a VQ-hierarchy inversion (§E).

**Notable patterns:**

- **Self-filling (primary):** EP-03a, EP-05a, EP-06a, EP-07a — all show substantial self-filling by the Kumada group in 1975–1978, consistent with the communication → Full Paper transition pattern noted in §6 of Stage 3. The Kumada group's research program (Kumada coupling full characterization) is the primary filling engine for this paper.

- **Paradigm-shift filling (decisive):** EP-03a, EP-04a, EP-06a — the most important filling event is not replication or incremental scope extension, but the paradigm shift from Ni(II)/Ni(II) to Ni(0)/Ni(II) catalytic cycle. This shift resolves three EPs simultaneously by re-framing the mechanistic questions entirely: the "self-contradictory absence" of R–R byproduct (CLA-1) is revealed to be a consequence of the incorrect cycle proposed in 1972, and the black precipitate is re-identified as the active Ni(0) catalyst rather than a decomposition product.

- **Technology-driven filling:** EP-03a, EP-06a — intermediate characterization was enabled by the maturation of ³¹P NMR spectroscopy and X-ray crystallography for air-sensitive Ni complexes in the 1974–1982 period; the analytical capability required to fill these EPs was not fully available in 1972.

- **Long-delay non-filling (VQ-4 inversion):** EP-08a and EP-09a remain unfilled as primary-paper contributions 54 years after the original paper, illustrating the VQ-hierarchy inversion pattern described in §E.

- **Comparison with author retrospective:** Tamao's later reviews (e.g., Tamao, K., *J. Organomet. Chem.* **653**, 23–26 (2002) — the retrospective mini-review noted in the CLAUDE.md as `papers/ep/discussion/tamao2002/`) describe the discovery in terms of the five significant features and the subsequent mechanistic elucidation. Notably, the retrospective does not discuss the self-contradictory absence of homo-coupling byproducts (CLA-1 / U-1) as a recognized gap — consistent with the UQS finding that CLA-1 was invisible from inside the field. The 2002 retrospective focuses on the positive scientific achievement (Ni(0)/Ni(II) cycle, stereospecificity) rather than on the reporting omissions, providing direct evidence that the structural absences identified by UQS were not visible to field insiders even in retrospect.

### §E Heuristic significance of unfilled EPs

**List of unfilled EPs:**

**EP-01a [Reporting-coherence pack]:**
