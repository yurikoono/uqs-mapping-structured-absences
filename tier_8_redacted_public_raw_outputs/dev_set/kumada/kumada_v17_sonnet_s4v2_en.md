The hierarchy–time hypothesis — [REDACTED: substantial verbatim quotation; see Tier 9] — is partially supported and partially inverted by this case. ### §E Heuristic significance of unfilled EPs

**List of unfilled EPs:**

**EP-07a [Boundary-test pack] — R–R homo-coupling byproduct quantification component:**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Misaligned incentive structure. The direct quantification of R–R homo-coupling byproduct yields for each Table I entry is technically straightforward (glpc with authentic R–R references under the same conditions) but has no publication incentive as an independent paper — reporting "byproduct yields from a 50-year-old method" does not constitute a novel finding under any current publication convention. The "selectivity" of Kumada coupling became established field knowledge without ever being formally quantified in the way the paper's own eq 3 predicted.
* **Heuristic significance**: yes — the selectivity of Ni-phosphine Grignard coupling is universally accepted as the field's consensus, yet the specific datum (R–R byproduct yield < X% per turnover for each substrate class) has never been reported as a primary finding in any paper; this is a case where a foundational quantitative claim is simultaneously universally accepted and unquantified in the primary literature, sustained by field consensus rather than by primary data.
* **Future filling feasibility**: high — the experiment is technically simple with modern glpc and GC-MS; it could be performed and reported as part of a method validation study or a mechanistic investigation without dedicated paper status; the more challenging question is publication incentive, not technical feasibility.

---

**EP-09a [Internal benchmark pack] — NiCl₂(dpe) vs. Ni(acac)₂ comparison (CLA-2 component):**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Misaligned incentive structure driven by concurrent-discovery dynamics. Comparing the Kumada (NiCl₂(dpe)) and Corriu–Masse (Ni(acac)₂) systems on matched substrate pairs was technically feasible in 1973 but was not in either group's interest to publish — such a paper would simultaneously acknowledge the competitor's method as valid and reduce the apparent uniqueness of the bidentate phosphine design. No third-party group had sufficient incentive to perform and publish this specific comparison. The field instead diverged: the Kumada system became the dominant form ("Kumada coupling") while Ni(acac)₂-based methods were noted but not systematically benchmarked against it.
* **Heuristic significance**: yes — the question [REDACTED: substantial verbatim quotation; see Tier 9] was structurally inaccessible from inside the field: both groups were simultaneously competing and unable to objectively compare, while outside observers lacked the publication incentive to make the comparison; this represents a case where the field's competitive structure prevented the most important comparative experiment from ever being formally published.
* **Future filling feasibility**: high — the matched-pair experiment (Table I substrates run in parallel with [NiCl₂(dpe)] and Ni(acac)₂) is technically trivial by modern standards and could be performed in a mechanistic or review context; the main barrier remains publication incentive, not feasibility.

---

**EP-08a [Replication / uncertainty pack]:**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Reporting-format issue compounded by field convention. Replicate yield data with uncertainty estimates for the 11 Table I entries of the 1972 communication were not reported in the 1976 BCSJ full paper (which reported expanded scope but not replicate runs for the original 11 entries) and will never be the subject of an independent paper. The field convention in synthetic organic chemistry does not require replicate yield data for scope tables in either communications or full papers; single-run yield data remain the standard.
* **Heuristic significance**: no — this absence is a universal reporting convention in synthetic chemistry and does not constitute a field-specific structural absence unique to the Kumada coupling; it carries no heuristic value specific to UQS analysis of this paper.
* **Future filling feasibility**: low — publication of replicate measurements for historical Table I entries as an independent paper is not feasible under any current publication convention; the only scenario in which this data would appear is as part of a large-scale benchmarking or method-validation study, where the 1972 paper's entries would be included incidentally.

**VQ-hierarchy inversion diagnosis:**

The filling pattern for the Kumada 1972 paper exhibits a partial VQ-hierarchy inversion. The low-VQ requirements (VQ-2: EP-01a reporting coherence, EP-07a scope boundary) were filled quickly (4 years) but via the automatic-resolution pathway (Communication→Full Paper), not via independent experimental papers focused on the missing data. The high-VQ requirements (VQ-3: EP-06a/EP-03a mechanism, EP-05a quantitative ranking) were also filled within 4 years for some sub-components (EP-05a via JACS 1976) — primarily because the Kumada group's research agenda (asymmetric coupling) generated VQ-3-level data as a byproduct. The VQ-4 requirements (EP-09a benchmark comparison, EP-08a replication) remain substantially unfilled.

The inversion is concentrated in the EP-09a (CLA-2) case: despite being a VQ-4-level benchmark requirement (RI-09), the Ni(acac)₂ vs. NiCl₂(dpe) comparison was never filled as an independent paper — not because it is technically difficult, but because the publication incentive structure of competitive research discouraged the comparison. This represents the most direct case in this analysis of the §E "misaligned incentive structure" pattern, where a VQ-4 requirement that is technically simpler than VQ-3 requirements (which were eventually filled) remains unfilled 52 years after the original paper.
