**VQ-hierarchy inversion diagnosis:**

A clear inversion is present and constitutes a UQS finding of field-level significance. The unfilled and least-well-filled EPs cluster at low VQ-2 requirements (EP-01a: RI-01 reporting coherence; EP-08a: RI-08 replication statistics), while the higher-VQ requirements (EP-03a: RI-03/RI-06, VQ-3 intermediate characterization; EP-06a: RI-06, VQ-3 cycle validation; EP-09a: RI-09, VQ-4 benchmark) received substantial filling through the Kumada coupling's mechanistic and comparative literature over the following decade.

In the Kumada coupling case, the field established the catalytic cycle's mechanistic validity (VQ-3), the phosphine ligand's structural role (VQ-3), and the comparative advantages over prior systems (VQ-4) — all high-VQ claims — while the low-VQ analytical foundation (yield-reporting coherence, replication statistics) remains permanently underspecified. The result is that the Kumada coupling's most fundamental quantitative claims are less well-evidenced in the published record than its most interpretive claims, a structural inversion that UQS reveals as a systematic property of publication-incentive-driven science.
