# EP-Filling Selection Map

## Murai, Kakiuchi, Sekine, Tanaka, Kamatani, Sonoda & Chatani 1993 (Nature, Article)

---

### §A Three-tier classification of EP filling difficulty

**Tier 1 (clearly trackable in the literature):** EP-03a, EP-04a, EP-06a, EP-07a, EP-10a

- **EP-03a [Direct-observation / state-assignment pack]:** The experiment demanded — stoichiometric cyclometallation of aromatic ketones with Ru complexes followed by NMR/X-ray characterisation of the metallacyclic intermediate, plus pre-catalyst activation spectroscopy — is precisely the main subject of mechanistic organometallic papers that the Chatani/Kakiuchi group and independent groups published as dedicated full papers between 1994 and 2005.
- **EP-04a [Discrimination / replication pack]:** Kinetic measurements on Ru-catalysed C–H functionalization (initial rates, time profiles, ring-strain effects, detection limits for regioisomers) appear as principal subjects in mechanistic full papers and dedicated kinetics studies; both the Murai group's own full papers and independent mechanistic investigations satisfy this tier.
- **EP-06a [Analogy-validation pack]:** Assessment of whether stoichiometric Mn(I)/Ru(II) cyclometallation is mechanistically equivalent to catalytic Ru(0) oxidative addition is the central question of multiple independent mechanistic papers on transition-metal-catalysed C–H activation; it is not buried in reviews.
- **EP-07a [Boundary-test pack]:** Testing non-carbonyl directing groups (amines, imines, phosphine oxides) to determine whether the chelation-assistance strategy requires a ketone specifically is the principal experimental design of many subsequent papers in directed C–H functionalization; these are readily trackable as independent papers.
- **EP-10a [Kinetic verification pack (provisional)]:** Measurement of kinetic isotope effects (kH/kD) for Ru-catalysed C–H activation, combined with rate-law derivation, is the main subject of dedicated mechanistic papers; the experiment is concrete and has been independently reproduced across multiple groups.

**Tier 2 (indirectly trackable):** EP-05a, EP-09a

- **EP-05a [Quantitative-ranking substantiation pack]:** Systematic regioisomer quantification for the Murai reaction using synthetic regioisomers as GC standards was not typically published as an independent paper; this data appears in the experimental sections of subsequent full papers by the Murai/Kakiuchi/Chatani group but is not the primary subject of any single independent paper.
- **EP-09a [Internal benchmark pack]:** Numerical TOF comparisons between the Murai reaction and prior-art systems (Jordan & Taylor 1989, Moore et al. 1992) are presented in review articles, benchmark papers, and textbook treatments of C–H functionalization rather than as independent experimental papers; the benchmark itself is not the main novelty claim of any single paper.

**Tier 3 (filling may not be found):** EP-01a, EP-08a

- **EP-01a [Reporting-coherence pack]:** The GC calibration protocols (internal standard, response factors) for the original 13-run scope table are a format-constrained RI-01 issue that was resolved partially in the Chatani/Kakiuchi group's subsequent full papers (JACS 1994, etc.) as a communication→full-paper transition, not as an independent paper.
- **EP-08a [Replication / uncertainty pack]:** Triplicate GC yield measurements with standard deviations for the Murai reaction runs are not available as independent papers; replication data at this level of statistical detail is not published as primary research in synthetic organometallic chemistry and follows the field convention of not publishing replicate measurement papers independently.

**EP-06a [Analogy-validation pack] (Tier 1)**

* **RI to be filled**: RI-06 [Imported model] (primary)
* **Filling Onset**: Ryabov, Alexander D. *Chemical Reviews* 1990, 90, 403–424. — Ryabov's comprehensive review of cyclopalladation and related electrophilic C–H activation processes (cited as ref 10 in the original paper) establishes the mechanistic distinction between electrophilic metalation (applicable to Pd, Pt, Mn) and oxidative addition (applicable to Rh, Ir, Ru(0)), providing the conceptual framework that allows assessment of the isomorphism question raised by EP-06a; although the review predates the Murai paper, it is the primary reference against which the Murai citation of refs 14/15 as analogies can be evaluated critically.
* **Filling Establishment**: Labinger, Jay A.; Bercaw, John E. *Nature* 2002, 417, 507–514. — Labinger and Bercaw's landmark mechanistic review of C–H activation by transition metals explicitly maps the oxidation-state-dependent mechanistic taxonomy (electrophilic metalation at Pd(II)/Mn(I) vs. oxidative addition at Rh(I)/Ir(I)/Ru(0)) and evaluates the conditions under which the analogies between different activation modes are structurally justified; this is the paper most directly filling EP-06a by providing the isomorphism (or lack thereof) assessment between electrophilic cyclometallation (refs 14/15 of the Murai paper) and Ru(0) OA.
* **Nature of filling**: Indirect (Labinger & Bercaw 2002 fills EP-06a at the conceptual level for the class of mechanisms — it establishes why the Murai analogy is mechanistically problematic — but does not perform the specific Mn(I)/Ru(II)-to-Ru(0) comparison for the Murai substrates; this remains for experimental papers).
* **Confidence**: High (Labinger & Bercaw 2002, Nature, is a well-known landmark review; Ryabov 1990 is cited in the original paper as ref 10).

The Morokuma group's DFT study of the Murai catalytic cycle (see also EP-10a) is a Tier 1 Technology-driven filling candidate for RI-06.

### §D Correspondence between VQ hierarchy and filling time order

**Filling time axis:**

- VQ-2 requirement (EP-01a, EP-04a — RI-01, RI-04/RI-05): 1995, Kakiuchi/Chatani/Murai group, JACS full paper. [Self-filling, automatic-resolution pattern]
- VQ-2 (scope) requirement (EP-07a — RI-07): 1995–1999, Kakiuchi/Murai group, Chemistry Letters / Synlett. [Self-filling, sequential scope expansion]
- VQ-3 requirement (EP-10a — PRI-11/RI-03 kinetic route): 1999–2001, Lenges/Brookhart + Weissman/Milstein, independent groups. [Technology-driven filling; new in-situ NMR and low-temperature kinetic methods]
- VQ-3 requirement (EP-03a — RI-03, intermediate observation): 2004, Kakiuchi/Kochi/Chatani group, JACS. [Self-filling, delayed by experimental difficulty]
- VQ-3 requirement (EP-06a — RI-06, analogy validation): 2002, Labinger/Bercaw, independent group. [Cross-field filling — mechanistic organometallic vs. synthetic chemistry]
- VQ-4 requirement (EP-09a — RI-09, benchmark): 2002, Kakiuchi/Murai, Accounts review. [Self-filling, retrospective review format]
- EP-05a (RI-05, regioisomer quantification): ~2001, Chatani/Murai group. [Self-filling]
- EP-08a (RI-08, replication): **unfilled** — field convention.

**Evaluation of hierarchy–time correspondence:**

The hierarchy–time hypothesis is substantially supported for the Murai 1993 case, with one notable inversion. VQ-2 requirements (RI-01, RI-04 at the analytical level) were resolved rapidly by the self-filling full paper in 1995 — within 2 years. VQ-3 requirements (RI-03 intermediate observation, RI-06 mechanistic analogy, PRI-11/RI-03 kinetic constraint) took 6–11 years to fill (1999–2004), and the filling involved independent groups with new computational and spectroscopic methods not available in 1993. VQ-4 requirements (RI-09 benchmark comparison) were addressed retrospectively ca. 2002, which is slightly earlier than might be expected — reflecting the fact that the benchmark comparison is retrospective in character (review article) rather than experimental, enabling an earlier publication date. The inversion: EP-07a (scope boundary, formally VQ-2 scope) was filled in 1995–1999, but the comprehensive directing-group taxonomy that fully satisfies EP-07a took until 2003–2010, spanning the full VQ-3 period — suggesting that "scope filling" cascades into VQ-3 territory when the boundary test generates mechanistic implications.

**Notable patterns:**

- **Self-filling dominance (VQ-2 and VQ-4):** All VQ-2 resolutions (EP-01a, EP-04a, EP-07a, EP-09a) were self-filled by the original Osaka University group. This is consistent with the communication→full-paper pattern: the omissions were format-constrained, and the original group was best positioned to provide the full experimental record.
- **Technology-driven filling (VQ-3, EP-10a):** The kinetic verification of the rate-determining step for the Murai reaction required in-situ spectroscopic methods and kinetic instrumentation that were not part of the Osaka group's synthetic methodology programme. The independent contributions of Brookhart (North Carolina) and Milstein (Weizmann Institute) reflect technology-driven filling from groups whose primary expertise was mechanistic organometallic chemistry rather than synthetic C–H functionalization.
- **Cross-field filling (VQ-3, EP-06a):** The mechanistic analogy critique (RI-06: imported Mn(I)/Ru(II) cyclometallation as equivalent to Ru(0) OA) was filled by the mechanistic organometallic chemistry community (Labinger/Bercaw) rather than by the synthetic chemistry community that produced the original paper. This is a cross-field filling: the question was identified within a synthetic context but resolved within a mechanistic context.
- **Long-delay filling (VQ-3, EP-03a):** Direct observation of the five-membered Ru metallacycle intermediate (the paper's central selectivity warrant) was not achieved until ca. 2004–2010, approximately 10–17 years after the original paper. This delay is consistent with the experimental difficulty of characterising a transient organometallic intermediate in a catalytic system: stoichiometric conditions were required (no olefin present) to accumulate enough of the metallacycle for NMR observation. The 1993 authors did not anticipate this experimental difficulty in the body of the Nature paper.
- **Bundle filling (EP-01a + EP-04a + EP-05a in the 1995 full paper):** The 1995 JACS full paper fills three EPs simultaneously. This is the largest bundle in the Murai 1993 filling landscape.
- **Comparison with author retrospective:** Kakiuchi and Murai's 2002 Accounts of Chemical Research review is the closest document to an author retrospective available. The review frames the subsequent research programme around substrate scope expansion (the N-3/N-5 programmatic claim: "applicable more widely") but does not explicitly acknowledge the mechanistic absences (U-1, U-2) from the 1993 paper. The structural absence of the active-species question (U-2: Ru(II) → Ru(0) activation) from the retrospective narrative is direct evidence that this question was "invisible to field insiders" — the original authors regarded the Ru(0) attribution as established by analogy rather than as an open question. EP-03a and EP-02a are precisely the EPs that the retrospective account does not mention, whereas EP-07a (directing-group scope) dominates the retrospective framing, confirming the UQS-visible / author-invisible asymmetry.

### §E Heuristic significance of unfilled EPs

**List of unfilled EPs:**

**EP-08a [Replication / uncertainty pack]:**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Misaligned incentive structure + field convention. In synthetic organometallic chemistry, replication of scope-table entries as independent triplicate experiments with standard deviations is not a publishable research unit; no journal in the field accepts a paper whose primary content is the statistical replication of previously reported yields. The format convention of the discipline explicitly excludes this form of uncertainty quantification from primary publications, and no independent group has ever published a replication study of the Murai reaction's original 13-run table with statistical characterisation. The 1995 JACS full paper expanded the scope but did not replicate the original 13 runs in triplicate.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes. EP-08a documents that the quantitative precision of a foundational scope-defining paper in synthetic chemistry — one with over 5,000 citations — is permanently uncharacterised at the statistical level. The question [REDACTED: substantial verbatim quotation; see Tier 9] has never been asked as a primary research question and cannot be filled under current publication conventions. This represents a structurally maintained absence: the field's incentive structure (novelty requirement for primary publications) ensures that the foundational quantitative claim of the Murai reaction will never receive the statistical characterisation that would be routine in, say, analytical chemistry or clinical research.
* **Future filling feasibility**: Medium. The experiment is technically trivial by modern standards (robotic synthesis platforms, automated GC sampling) and could be performed as a data paper or supplementary dataset for a methods comparison study. The barrier is not technical but incentive-structural: the result would be publishable only if framed within a broader methodological context (e.g., a cross-laboratory reproducibility study of landmark synthetic methodology papers, or as a benchmark dataset for robotic synthesis).

**EP-09a [Internal benchmark pack] (partially unfilled):**

* **Reason for non-filling**: Misaligned incentive structure + technical impossibility of direct comparison. The most direct filling of EP-09a — a side-by-side TOF measurement comparing the Murai (Ru/aromatic ketone/olefin), Jordan & Taylor (Zr/α-picoline/propene), and Moore (Ru/pyridine/CO/hexene) systems on a common substrate — is technically impossible because the three systems require distinct substrate classes and conditions. The substrate incompatibility means the benchmark comparison can only be performed retrospectively by normalisation and approximation (as in review articles), not by direct experiment. An independent paper performing this comparison as its primary result has never been published, and there is no incentive to publish it.
* **Heuristic significance**: Yes. The TOF superiority claim of Murai 1993 — "among the highest reported for this type of reaction" — was never numerically grounded in the original paper and remains numerically ungrounded in any primary paper. The claim has propagated through the literature (cited in reviews and subsequent papers as established) without ever being supported by the tabulated numerical comparison that would constitute formal EP-09a filling. This is a case of a quantitative claim that is both foundational (it underpins the efficiency novelty claim N-1) and structurally unfillable in primary paper form. It is therefore simultaneously a VQ-4 failure that has been treated by the field as a VQ-4 success — a heuristic finding of the first order.
* **Future filling feasibility**: Low. The substrate incompatibility between the three compared systems means that a direct experimental benchmark filling cannot be designed. Computational comparison (DFT-derived TOF predictions for each system under a unified mechanistic model) is theoretically possible but has never been performed. The most feasible filling is a meta-analysis in a review article, which cannot constitute an EP filling under the Stage 3/4 definition (reviews do not fill EPs).

**VQ-hierarchy inversion diagnosis:**

A partial inversion is documented for Murai 1993. EP-08a (RI-08, replication) and EP-09a (RI-09, external benchmark) are low-VQ requirements in the sense that they concern the analytical infrastructure and external comparison standard of the claims — precisely the infrastructure that should be established first, before mechanism and scope are addressed. However, both remain unfilled (EP-08a) or only indirectly filled (EP-09a) thirty years after the original paper, while the high-VQ requirements — EP-03a (RI-03, intermediate observation), EP-06a (RI-06, analogy validation), EP-10a (PRI-11, kinetics) — were addressed within 6–17 years by independent groups. The actual filling order in the Murai case is therefore:

**Observed filling order:** VQ-2 (RI-01/RI-04/RI-07 scope, self-filling, 2 years) → VQ-3 (RI-06/PRI-11/RI-03, technology/cross-field filling, 6–11 years) → VQ-4 (RI-09, retrospective review, 9 years) → RI-08 (unfilled, 31+ years) → RI-09 experimental (unfilled in primary paper form, 31+ years)

**Logical filling order:** RI-01/RI-08 (analytical verification) → RI-09 (benchmark) → RI-04/RI-05 (kinetics) → RI-03/RI-06/PRI-11 (mechanistic)

The inversion is confirmed: the field filled the high-VQ mechanistic and scope requirements (EP-03a, EP-06a, EP-07a, EP-10a) before filling the low-VQ analytical infrastructure requirements (EP-08a, EP-09a). The incentive structure reward is unambiguous — mechanism papers and scope extension papers generate independent novelty claims; replication and benchmark comparison papers do not. This is the strongest structural absence finding from Stage 4 for Murai 1993: the most verifiable and technically simple unfilled EP (EP-08a) remains unfilled precisely because it is too simple and too devoid of novelty to generate a primary publication.
