**Structural suppression vs. individual choice**

RP-1 (mechanism validation):
- *Era-dependent*: structurally suppressed (1972: no radical probes, no DFT, no in situ spectroscopy) → individually chosen / methodologically limited (2014: radical clock + DFT deployed, DFT precision acknowledged as insufficient) → field-norm sediment (2015: DFT-without-experiment becomes review-scale convention) → individually chosen (2024: CV, SWV, kinetics, DFT, EPR-feasible all available; EPR not deployed).

RP-2 (analytical underreporting):
- *Incentive-suppressed*: Publishing replicate kinetic data, GC conversion alongside isolated yields, statistical uncertainty on rate orders, or unified reference-electrode protocols would not constitute publishable contributions. The incentive structure of chemical journals (synthetic = scope; mechanistic = cycle proposals; review = coverage; electrochemical = applicability) does not reward this work.

RP-3 (DFT as sole discriminant):
- *Individually chosen*: All three post-DFT papers had access to dispersion-corrected functionals (since 2010), quasi-RRHO entropy correction (since Grimme 2012), DLPNO-CCSD(T) benchmarks (since 2013), and BS-DFT spin-correction methodology (established since the 1990s). Their non-deployment is individually chosen.

RP-4 (self-contradictory absence):
- *Individually chosen*: Each untested prediction is testable. Testing R–R formation (1972) requires only glpc. Testing intermediates 10–16 (2014) requires stoichiometric NMR + EPR. Testing Ni¹/Ni³ (any post-2010 paper) requires freeze-quench EPR. Testing the homocoupling distribution (2024) requires the same selectivity model already embedded in the proposal but algebraically derived. None of these requires a different genre of paper.

RP-5 (mass balance):
- *Incentive-suppressed*: A comprehensive mass-balance audit of Ni-catalyzed cross-coupling (cross + homo + protodehalogenation + dehalogenated dimer + recovered SM, all quantified) would constitute a standalone analytical paper that no high-impact journal would publish. No group in the field has published such a study across 52 years.

RP-6 (external benchmarking):
- *Era-dependent*: structurally suppressed (1972: head-to-head comparison required substrate access from competing groups, often not feasible) → incentive-suppressed (2014–2024: comparison is feasible but would consume scope-table real estate without adding novelty; reviewers do not request it).

**Succession relationship:**
Paradigm B's founding problem (Csp³ coupling) is precisely Paradigm A's acknowledged blind spot. Paradigm C's organizing principle (Ni¹/Ni³ unifying mechanism) systematizes Paradigm B's individual mechanistic proposals. Paradigm D's substrate space (fully unactivated alkyl halides) is the boundary that Paradigms B and C had not crossed (Paradigm B requires aryl Grignard partner; Paradigm C requires Csp³–Csp² disconnection). Each paradigm's founding problem is the previous paradigm's blind spot. **This is paradigm succession, not competition** — by 2014 no group proposes a 2-electron mechanism for Csp³ alkyl halides; by 2024 no group proposes a stoichiometric-reductant approach for "general" Csp³–Csp³ coupling.

**The paradigm-invariant blind spot:** No paradigm quantifies cross- vs. homo-coupling selectivity through closed mass balance. Kumada 1972 reports no R–R; Breitenfeld 2014 acknowledges no homocoupling data; Cherney 2015 inherits from primary papers; Pengfei 2024 reports 17%+13% homocoupling without reconciling with the proposed selectivity model. **Mass-balance closure is paradigm-invariant absent.**

**Cherney 2015's review function**: maps the succession by classifying enantioselective vs. enantiospecific and surfaces SRG-2's mechanistic gap (Ni¹/Ni³ without EPR), but does not impose the missing measurement requirement on the field — the review reproduces the convention rather than enforcing a new one. This is consistent with **Field-consensus overread** (PRI-12 candidate per Cherney S3): a review-scale pattern that creates the appearance of mechanistic consensus where the experimental basis is absent.
